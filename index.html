01 / Research

The social brain,
in a changing world.

My work asks how we understand other minds, experience control over our actions, and respond to the people and technologies around us.

At McMaster, I work with Prof. Milena Head and Prof. Khaled Hassanein, bringing social cognitive neuroscience into conversation with human–AI interaction.

01

Empathy &
social cognition

How we respond to others’ experiences, including pain, and how social context shapes those responses.

02

Agency &
imitation

How we experience our own actions, coordinate with others, and come to feel in control, individually and together.

03

Human–AI
interaction

How our cognition is affected by AI use, how we mentally represent AI as social agents, and how we can optimize human–AI interactions in the workplace.

Across methods

EEG · TMS · Psychophysiology · Behavioural experiments · Quantitative modeling
