research 3 threads

My work asks how we understand other minds, experience control over our actions, and respond to the people and technologies around us.

empathy/ · 14 papers

How we respond to others’ experiences, including pain, and how social context shapes those responses.

e.g. Please Empathize! (2020), social power & ERPs of empathy for pain (2021)

agency-control/ · 16 papers

How we experience our own actions, coordinate with others, and come to feel in control, individually and together.

e.g. Sense of agency during group control (2025), Anticipated imitation of multiple agents (2024)

human-ai/ · 3 papers

How AI use affects cognition, how we mentally represent AI as social agents, and how human–AI interaction can be improved.

e.g. The face of ChatGPT (preprint), Multimodal LLMs encode implicit gender bias (under review)

methods: EEG · TMS · psychophysiology · behavioural experiments · quantitative modelling
